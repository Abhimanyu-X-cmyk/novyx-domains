Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. Before Registration

If payment is received but the domain has not been registered, you may request cancellation and a refund.

2. Failed Registration

If registration fails and no domain is registered, the payment for that registration will be refunded.

3. Completed Registrations

Completed domain registrations and renewals are generally non-refundable, subject to registrar rules and applicable consumer-protection laws.

4. Duplicate Payments

Verified duplicate payments will be refunded.

5. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds will be initiated within 7–10 business days. The actual time may vary by the payment provider.

6. Contact

For refund requests, contact our support team using the official contact details published on our website.
